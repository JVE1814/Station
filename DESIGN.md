# Simulator interface

The [view study](reference/view-study.png) sets the visual direction for the world across daylight, night and snow. The rendered scene leads. The cab instruments use round dials and warm illumination, with operating controls grouped within reach of the driver's seat.

Keep the screen's center clear. Provide a persistent cluster for current speed, line limit, next stop and distance, with small keyboard hints beside the camera controls. Use restrained charcoal surfaces, off-white text, and amber for advisory states. Reserve red and green for signal and interlock state. Use tabular numerals for speed, distance and time.
