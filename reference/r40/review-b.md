# Round 1

I reviewed all 14 frames at 1920 × 1080 against a premium commercial train simulator standard. **07 Lake Overlook Night scored lowest at 2.92/10.** The near-vertical camera reduces the train to a faint orange line, most of the terrain approaches black, and a hard triangular headlight beam is the only prominent subject. **04 River Bridge Golden scored highest at 6.02/10.** Its strong rail diagonal, layered hills, and low sun give the scene scale, but the shore and bridge materials still look unfinished.

The most consequential defects are the lake seams and distorted reflections, the hard-edged night beams, the rectangular snowfall footprint in 07, and the disconnected passenger limbs in 06. The overhead views also need a lower angle or a tighter frame to keep the train legible and show the depth of the valley.
